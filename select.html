What Einheit Select Is

Einheit Select is a curated, invitation-reviewed division within the Einheit Collective. It exists to recognize fictional phone brands with a distinct creative point of view — not to serve as a second entry point for brands that did not initially qualify for the standard collective.

Review Process

Every application is read in full by the selection committee. There is no guaranteed acceptance timeline, and incomplete or low-effort submissions are declined without individual feedback. Applications may be re-submitted no earlier than 30 days after a decline.

Expectations of Members

Select members are expected to maintain a consistent creative output, uphold the visual and narrative quality that earned their acceptance, and represent the division professionally across public and community spaces. Select status is reviewed periodically and is not permanent.
